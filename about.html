About Us

History: We launched Helios & Co. after watching too many classmates get locked into expensive year-long contracts for spaces that looked nothing like their online photos. College life moves fast, and student housing should keep up.

Mission: Make off-campus renting straightforward and flexible. We curate affordable semester leases, private rooms, and shared apartments that actually fit an academic calendar and a student budget.

Team: Our crew works side-by-side with local property owners, campus housing staff, and student renters to verify listings, answer questions honestly, and eliminate move-in surprises.
